# Deferred Cinematic Assets — Production Briefs

These are intentionally *not* represented by fake placeholder photography.

## HERO_ENVIRONMENT_DESKTOP
**Target:** 3840×2160 master, AVIF/WebP delivery.
Minimal near-black architectural void; a single abstract AI Core suspended in a huge physical space.
No Iron-Man HUD aesthetic. Contemporary product photography + installation art + computational light.
Large negative space. Cold diffuse light with one restrained warm reflection.
Must support text on left and interactive WebGL Core in center/right.

## HERO_ENVIRONMENT_MOBILE
**Target:** 1440×1920.
Same art direction, vertical composition, lower particle density and clearer silhouette.

## INTRO_FILM
**Duration:** 3–5 seconds, seamless entry into the live WebGL Core.
Black → faint soundless energy → Core surface becomes visible → camera settles → live Core replaces film.
No logo animation longer than ~700 ms. Skippable. Respect reduced-motion.

## MEMORY_ENVIRONMENT
A dark 3D field of temporal connections rather than a generic "brain".
Node groups: People / Projects / Places / Documents / Events.
Old facts remain visible as dim historical edges.

## PHYSICAL_WORLD
Architectural cutaway / device constellation showing PC, phone, room and sensors.
Avoid smart-home stock imagery.

## AUDIO_IDENTITY
Produce separately with an audio tool:
- 2–3 second boot motif
- 250 ms wake signature
- listening loop without annoyance
- completion signature
- warning tone
The sound must be subtle enough for daily use.
